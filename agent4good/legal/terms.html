Terms of use

For the single-owner, self-hosted application.

Owner use

Agent4Good is a self-hosted operator for one owner. Access requires the password configured by the person running this installation. There is no public signup or shared-account service.

Your responsibilities

Use only data, accounts, and systems you have permission to use. Keep your password and provider credentials private. Review permissions, budgets, and approval requests before allowing external actions.

Results and external actions

AI output can be wrong or incomplete. Check results before relying on them. Automated checks cover their configured assertions only. Cancelling work stops future steps; an external request already in progress may still finish.

Connected services

You choose which model providers and tools to configure. Their terms and charges apply separately. Agent4Good does not promise that third-party services will remain available.

Hosting and saved work

The person running this installation manages hosting, backups, and access. Stopping the service or cancelling a task does not erase saved work. Export needed records before arranging deletion.

Support

For product support, email appwrite@mail.tin.computer. Do not send passwords, API keys, or private task contents. For installation access or data removal, contact the person running your installation.
